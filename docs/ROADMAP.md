# Roadmap

## 0.1 complete-loop target

- native scenario and operator model;
- deployment, simple movement/terrain, radius visibility;
- deterministic direct attack and simultaneous damage;
- elimination, objective-score and explicit truncation;
- stable Session/Agent/local-tools APIs;
- checksummed replay, restore and replay verification.

## Later, only as explicit native rulesets

- polygon/hex terrain and deterministic path search;
- elevation and occlusion models;
- richer weapon/ammunition models;
- communications, electronic warfare and logistics;
- benchmark adapters, branch-tree tooling and optional PyQt views;
- performance profiling and selective native-code acceleration.

No roadmap item implies reproduction of hidden or restricted official rules.
